Hi, I’m Alex.

Father of 3. Founder at ScaleX.
Human. Based in Detroit.

Most of my life, I’ve been trying to understand what was wrong with me—and chasing the love and acceptance I couldn’t give myself.

Through grit, shameless ambition and a lot of luck, I built a life that looked pretty good on paper. Living inside it was another story.

I’m a guy from Detroit with ADHD, a fucked-up sense of humor, and a lot still to figure out.

A little of the actual story

At 31,
I wasn’t okay.

Shortly after my son was born, I felt hopeless and paralyzed by anxiety and depression. I was ready to check myself into a psych ward. That wasn’t what my life looked like from the outside.

My son’s love gave me a reason to start therapy. At first, I showed up out of desperation. Sometimes twice a week. It became some of the hardest and most worthwhile work I’ve ever done.

Divorce, fatherhood, and years of therapy have changed how I see myself. I’ve had to let go of a lot of the person I thought I was supposed to be.

I’m grateful for the life I have. I also still struggle with anxiety, depression, and an ADHD brain that doesn’t reliably cooperate with the plan. Both things are true.

Why I write

It helps me
make sense of things.

Writing makes me feel good. So does the idea that somebody might spend five minutes reading something I have to say. There are probably less selfish reasons, too.

I write about happiness, burnout, ambition, and the things I’m still trying to understand about myself. If somebody recognizes a little of their own life in it, that means something to me.
